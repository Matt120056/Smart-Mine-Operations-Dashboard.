Smart Mine Operations Dashboard

-- Design Overview --
The dashboard is designed as a central monitoring system for mine operators. It should provide a quick overview of the mine's current conditions, equipment, connected devices, and safety concerns.

The main screen should include:
- System and device status
- Environmental sensor readings
- Equipment activity and status
- Safety alerts

The layout should make imporant information easy to identify at a glance, with sensor readings and device status grouped together, equipment activity represented separately, and alerts clearly visible.

The wireframe should remain low-fidelity and focus on the organization of information ratheer than visualy styling.
