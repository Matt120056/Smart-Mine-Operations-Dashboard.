# Smart Mine Operations Dashboard

STORYBOARD — Automated Mine Ventilation & Safety Dashboard
Role: Storyteller
Persona: Matt, a ventilation/ IT technician at an underground nickel mine

--------------------------------------------------------
PANEL 1 — The Problem
--------------------------------------------------------
Setting: Mine surface control room, early morning.
Action: Matt sits at a desk surrounded by radios and paper logs.
A radio crackles with a level supervisor asking whether it's
safe to send a crew back into a section after a blast.
Thought bubble: "I'm guessing based on a stopwatch and a gut feeling."
Purpose: Establishes the problem — no real-time visibility into
air quality, and re-entry decisions rely on manual timing/radio
check-ins instead of actual sensor data.

--------------------------------------------------------
PANEL 2 — Opening the Dashboard
--------------------------------------------------------
Setting: Same control room, now with a tablet/monitor showing
the Grafana dashboard.
Action: Matt pulls up the live view — gas concentration,
airflow, and fan status for every level, updating in real time.
Thought bubble: "Now I can actually see what's happening down there."
Purpose: Introduces the app and its core value — real-time,
level-by-level visibility instead of guesswork.

--------------------------------------------------------
PANEL 3 — A Blast Event Fires
--------------------------------------------------------
Setting: Dashboard view, mid-shift.
Action: A blast event alert appears on screen. The ventilation
fans on that level automatically spike to full speed on their
own — Matt watches the fan-speed panel jump without touching
anything.
Caption: "The system already reacted before I even saw the alert."
Purpose: Shows the automation (VOD) doing its job — forcing
ventilation immediately after a blast, without waiting on a
human to notice and respond.

--------------------------------------------------------
PANEL 4 — Watching the Air Clear
--------------------------------------------------------
Setting: Close-up on the dashboard's gas-concentration graph
and a "re-entry" status indicator.
Action: Matt watches the CO/CO2 trend line fall in real time
toward the safe threshold. A status panel counts down from
"CLEARING" to "SAFE TO ENTER."
Caption: "I don't have to guess when it's safe — the data tells me."
Purpose: Demonstrates the safety payoff — a data-backed
re-entry decision instead of a fixed, conservative wait time
or a risky early guess.

--------------------------------------------------------
PANEL 5 — Giving the All-Clear
--------------------------------------------------------
Setting: Matt back on the radio, dashboard still visible
beside her.
Action: he radios the crew: "Level 2 is clear, gas levels are
back to normal, you're good to head back in."
Purpose: Closes the loop — the technology directly enables a
faster, safer real-world decision and action.

--------------------------------------------------------
PANEL 6 — Looking Back at the Trends
--------------------------------------------------------
Setting: End of shift, Matt reviewing a historical chart on
the dashboard.
Action: Matt notices Level 2 consistently spikes higher after
blasts than other levels, and flags it for a booster fan
upgrade in that section.
Caption: "The data's showing me patterns I'd never have caught
from memory alone."
Purpose: Shows the longer-term value of the historian/dashboard
— not just real-time safety, but data-driven planning and
continuous improvement.

--------------------------------------------------------
